**Your old exam paper is someone's best revision tonight.**

Every semester, juniors hunt for last year's question papers in WhatsApp groups, in blurry photos, and in seniors' old notebooks.

We built **PyQdeck** so that none of that gets lost. It's a app with previous-year papers, solved questions, and study notes, organized by subject and year and topic.

But we can't cover every paper alone, and you can help. In v1.13 you can now **contribute a paper straight from the app**:

📸 Take a photo, pick one from your gallery, or attach a PDF
⏱️ It takes under a minute
🎓 It helps the next batch prepare

Got a paper from a past exam, even a half-torn one? Pass it on. The next batch will thank you.

Seniors, this one is for you. Juniors, share this with your batch. 🙌

👉 Download PyQdeck: [Play Store link]
Open the app → Home → **Upload Exam Paper**

### Option A: single hero image (recommended)

- **Size:** 1200 x 1350 px (4:5 portrait). It takes the most feed space on mobile and stays readable.
- **Headline (big, top):** "Your old paper is someone's best revision tonight."
- **Centre:** a phone mockup showing the Home screen with the **Upload Exam Paper** card in focus (the pass-it-on text visible). Capture it on a real device, light theme.
- **Supporting visual:** a few small paper/photo thumbnails floating toward the phone, to say "pass it on".
- **Bottom strip:** PyQdeck logo (`assets/pyqdeck-logo.svg`) and "Upload Exam Paper: Home -> Upload" in small text. No Play Store URL on the image; put the link in the post.
- **Style:** the PyQdeck logo colours (red `#C6322A`, teal `#214440`, navy `#0D192B` on the warm off-white `#FAF8F6`) and the app fonts, so it reads as PyQdeck. Max two font sizes.
